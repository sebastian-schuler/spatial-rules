## Question

- Property tests over synthetic overlapping rule sets: the winner is the max-priority applicable rule; tie-break is deterministic (declaration order); values are first-provider-wins; the result is stable across repeat evaluations and across both spatial-index kinds (RStar vs LinearScan).
